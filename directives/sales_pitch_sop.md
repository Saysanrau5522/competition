## 2. The 3-Bullet Sales Closing Cheat Sheet Format

When an inbound lead is submitted via the advisor portal, the backend generates an actionable 3-bullet cheat sheet for the assigned sales representative:

### Format Structure:
1. **Bullet 1 — The Empathetic Hook:**
   - Mention the business owner's industry, company size, and specific stated hours wasted per week (e.g., *"Hi [Owner Name], I saw you run an 8-person retail team in Penang and you're currently losing ~15 hours every week manually chasing WhatsApp orders."*).
2. **Bullet 2 — The Phased Exabytes Prescription:**
   - Propose the exact Phase 1 quick win product that addresses their biggest pain point immediately without heavy capital expenditure.
   - Example: *"Before we look at complex CRM setups, our top recommendation is deploying Exabytes AI Website Builder with integrated WhatsApp automation so your orders flow directly into one dashboard."*
3. **Bullet 3 — The Mathematical Justification (Deterministic ROI):**
   - Quote their personalized calculated savings in Ringgit Malaysia based on their input.
   - Example: *"Based on Malaysian administrative averages (RM25/hr), reclaiming those 15 hours will recover over RM 13,650 annually in direct labor costs, paying for the RM 358/yr package within the first 10 days."*

## 3. Objection Handling Guide for Exabytes Reps

- **Objection 1: "We are too small, we don't need AI or expensive IT."**
  - *Response:* "That's exactly why we designed Phase 1 for quick wins. Our entry solution is under RM 30/month. We don't ask you to spend thousands upfront; we start by saving you 5 hours a week."
- **Objection 2: "My staff are not tech-savvy and won't know how to use it."**
  - *Response:* "Exabytes provides 24x7x365 local Malaysian support in English, Malay, and Mandarin. Our onboarding specialists configure the initial setup for your team."
- **Objection 3: "We already have a WhatsApp number and a Facebook page."**
  - *Response:* "Social media and WhatsApp are great for chatting, but without automated backup and structured lead routing, you lose ~25% of inquiries that message after 8 PM. We connect directly into your existing channels without forcing your staff to learn a new system."
