# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): SME Digital Maturity & AI Readiness Diagnostic

## Purpose
This document defines the mathematical benchmarks, grading criteria, dynamic question triggers, and Malaysian economic formulas used by the AI SME Digital Growth Advisor.

---

## 1. Six Pillars of Digital Maturity (0 - 100 Score)

Each SME is graded across 6 core pillars. The maximum aggregate score is 100 points:

| Pillar | Focus Area | Max Score | Benchmark Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Web & Digital Presence** | Domain, professional email, responsive website, e-commerce readiness | 20 pts | Personal email (@gmail): 3 pts. Static site: 10 pts. Custom domain + SSL + lead capture / e-commerce: 20 pts. |
| **2. Cloud Infrastructure & Storage** | Data accessibility, off-site cloud backup, centralized file management | 20 pts | Local USB/hard drive only: 3 pts. Unsynced Google Drive/Dropbox: 10 pts. Automated cloud backup + remote VPS: 20 pts. |
| **3. CRM & Customer Tracking** | Lead capture, follow-up automation, contact lifecycle management | 15 pts | Paper notebooks / WhatsApp chat log: 2 pts. Excel spreadsheet: 7 pts. Cloud CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, etc.): 15 pts. |
| **4. Marketing & Automation** | Social media scheduling, programmatic email campaigns, automated booking | 15 pts | Ad-hoc manual posts: 3 pts. Basic Facebook/Instagram page: 7 pts. Automated workflows + newsletter funnels: 15 pts. |
| **5. Cybersecurity & Compliance** | SSL certificates, ransomware defense, data protection (PDPA Malaysia) | 15 pts | No protection: 2 pts. Basic antivirus: 7 pts. Enterprise Acronis cloud backup + multi-layer security: 15 pts. |
| **6. AI Readiness & Process Agility** | Standardized digital data, documented workflows, AI tool adoption | 15 pts | Pure manual paper processes: 2 pts. Casual ChatGPT usage: 7 pts. Documented data pipelines + AI copilots integrated: 15 pts. |

### Maturity Classification Tiers
- **0 - 35: Digital Novice (High Vulnerability / High Upside)**
  - Urgent priority: Stabilize foundational digital presence and secure against data loss.
- **36 - 65: Digital Practitioner (Productivity Bottlenecks)**
  - Urgent priority: Unify siloed tools, implement centralized CRM, and automate repetitive customer messaging.
- **66 - 85: Digital Accelerator (Poised for AI Scale)**
  - Urgent priority: Deploy AI chatbots, predictive sales outreach, and workflow automation.
- **86 - 100: Digital Leader (Optimization & Modernization)**
  - Urgent priority: Custom AI agents, enterprise-grade cloud resilience, and full-stack cybersecurity.

## 2. Deterministic Malaysian Economic ROI Math

To prevent AI hallucination and build executive trust, all financial projections use deterministic Malaysian SME salary benchmarks (Department of Statistics Malaysia & SME Corp averages):

- **Baseline Administrative Hourly Rate:** RM 25.00 / hour (approx. RM 4,200 - RM 4,800/mo fully loaded cost for office executive/admin).
- **Executive / Owner Time Valuation:** RM 60.00 / hour.
- **Annual Operational Weeks:** 52 weeks.

### Core Calculation Formulas

1. **Annual Administrative Hours Saved:**
   $$\text{Hours Saved/Year} = \text{Weekly Hours Wasted} \times 0.70 \times 52$$
   *(Assuming technology recovers ~70% of manual repetitive task overhead).*

2. **Annual Direct Cash Recovered:**
   $$\text{Direct Cash Saved} = \text{Annual Hours Saved} \times \text{RM 25.00/hr}$$

3. **Lead Leakage / Customer Attrition Recovery:**
   If the user notes customer inquiries or quote delays as a bottleneck:
   - Estimated monthly missed opportunities: 5 to 20 inquiries.
   - Average SME ticket size: RM 350.00.
   - Lead recovery factor with instant response: 25%.
   $$\text{Annual Recovered Revenue} = (\text{Monthly Inquiries} \times 0.25 \times \text{RM 350}) \times 12$$

4. **Net ROI Ratio:**
   $$\text{Estimated 12-Month Net Value} = \text{Direct Cash Saved} + \text{Annual Recovered Revenue}$$
   $$\text{ROI Multiple} = \frac{\text{Estimated 12-Month Net Value}}{\text{Exabytes Annual Solution Cost}}$$

## 3. Dynamic Question Logic Matrix

When the user selects an industry and primary bottleneck, the system dynamically injects a targeted follow-up question:

| Industry Sector | Primary Bottleneck | Dynamic Follow-up Question | Intent / Data Extracted |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Retail / E-Commerce** | Manual Order Tracking / Stock | *"How many orders per day are processed manually through WhatsApp or physical receipts?"* | Quantifies fulfillment overhead for e-commerce automation. |
| **Professional Services** | Client Invoicing & Follow-up | *"How many hours does your team spend weekly chasing client approvals, invoices, and scheduling?"* | Direct input for administrative hour recovery formula. |
| **F&B / Hospitality** | Customer Retention & Reviews | *"Do you currently store customer phone numbers/emails for repeat promotions or loyalty programs?"* | Identifies CRM and marketing automation readiness. |
| **Manufacturing / Logistics** | Data Loss & Inventory Tracking | *"If your central computer or server failed today, how long would it take to restore operations?"* | Justifies Acronis Cloud Backup & Business Continuity. |
| **Healthcare / Wellness** | Appointment Scheduling & No-Shows | *"What percentage of appointment bookings are managed manually via phone calls or walk-ins?"* | Justifies automated booking engine & WhatsApp AI agent. |
| *Default / Other* | Customer Inquiries Overload | *"Roughly how many customer inquiries go unanswered or take longer than 2 hours during peak times?"* | Measures lead leakage for 24/7 AI chatbot justification. |

## 4. 12-Month Transformation Roadmap Phases

1. **Phase 1: Quick Wins (Months 1–3) — Foundational Stability**
   - Professional Business Email (@yourcompany.com.my)
   - Mobile-responsive digital storefront with SSL
   - Automated Daily Cloud Backup (Acronis)
2. **Phase 2: Operational Productivity (Months 3–6) — Workflow Unification**
   - Cloud CRM contact management
   - Omnichannel WhatsApp & Web inquiry dashboard
   - Team collaboration & automated billing
3. **Phase 3: Automated Scaled Growth (Months 6–12) — AI & Autonomy**
   - 24/7 AI Customer Support Bot (trained on SME products/FAQs)
   - Automated email/WhatsApp nurture sequences
   - Predictive sales analytics & advanced cloud server scaling
